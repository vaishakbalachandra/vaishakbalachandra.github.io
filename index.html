Estimation · Regimes · Robust decisions

Vaishak Balachandra

I study what can be inferred about something from the part of it you are allowed to see. In markets, that means estimating hidden states, and the hidden regimes they move between, from prices, company filings and ownership data, and then deciding how to act on an estimate that is never fully trustworthy.

01

Research

Hidden states, hidden regimes

A price is a noisy observation of something underneath it: what a company is worth, what a crowd believes, what a few people already know. A Kalman filter can estimate a hidden state like that, but only because it knows the rules by which the state moves. Markets do not offer that comfort. Their rules change, usually without announcing it, and a model that worked yesterday can be quietly wrong today.

My research interest has two parts.

  1. Estimation and detectionPrices are a fast channel; company filings and ownership disclosures are slow, sparse and partly written in words. Can a model that reads both detect a change in a company's regime before the price reflects it, and what, measurably, separates the two channels when they disagree?
  2. DecisionOnce such an estimate exists, with an error attached and some probability that the regime has already moved, how should capital be allocated on it when the model itself may be wrong?
  • Latent-state estimation
  • Regime switching
  • Mixed-frequency data
  • Optimal stopping
  • Robust optimization
  • Partial observability

Research projects

Privacy by architecture for retrieval-augmented generation

M.S. thesis · 2025–2026
Problem
Retrieval systems for language models encode every sensitive attribute of a record together and add protection afterwards, so information can leak across attributes.
Idea
Split each record by attribute before encoding, so that no single component ever observes a whole record, and recombine only at the end.
Result
Of four systems tested on synthetic medical records, three gradually exhausted their privacy budget; the multi-model design kept all of it, with faithfulness comparable to the baseline.
